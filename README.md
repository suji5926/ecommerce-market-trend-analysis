
# E-commerce Market Trend Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a dataset containing 10,000 e-commerce orders to explore sales performance, customer purchasing patterns, product categories and market trends.

The objective is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business information using data cleaning, SQL analysis, Python and interactive Power BI visualizations.

## Business Objectives

- Examine sales and revenue-related metrics across products and categories.
- Explore customer purchasing patterns and order characteristics.
- Analyze sales trends over time and across customer segments.
- Investigate relationships between discounts, order quantities, costs and net amounts.
- Explore market indicators such as search interest, competitor pricing, market growth and advertising expenditure.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data inspection, cleaning and preparation.
- **SQL:** Business queries, aggregations, grouping and analytical calculations.
- **Python (Pandas):** Data processing, validation and exploratory analysis.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and business performance visualization.

## Dataset Overview

The dataset contains 10,000 e-commerce orders and includes fields related to:

- Orders, customers and order dates
- Product categories and brands
- Order status, payment method and shipping cost
- Discounts, coupons and quantities
- Gross amount, total cost and net amount
- Customer demographics and acquisition channels
- Search interest, competitor prices and market growth indicators

## Project Workflow

1. **Data inspection and cleaning:** Examined the dataset structure, checked data types and prepared the data for analysis.
2. **SQL analysis:** Developed queries to summarize business metrics and investigate sales, customer and market-related questions.
3. **Python analysis:** Used Pandas for data handling, validation and supporting analytical calculations.
4. **Power BI visualization:** Developed an interactive dashboard to present key metrics and trends.
5. **Business interpretation:** Used analytical outputs to identify patterns and generate insights relevant to e-commerce performance.

## Key Analysis Areas

- Sales and revenue performance
- Product and category comparisons
- Customer and order patterns
- Discount and purchasing behavior
- Monthly and seasonal trends
- Market indicators and competitor pricing
